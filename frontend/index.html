📖 User manual

Book Recommender finds books for you by comparing them against the books you already like. Everything below is optional — but the more you save and rate, the better the recommendations get.

Quick start
  1. Create an account with Log in → Sign up (top right).
  2. Search for a few books you love and click Save to Library.
  3. Open My Library and hit Get Recommendations Based on My Library.
👤 Accounts
  • Sign up / log in with the button in the top-right corner. Usernames are 2–32 characters, passwords at least 6.
  • Your library follows your account, so it's the same on your phone and your laptop. You stay signed in on a device until you log out.
  • You can search and use Find Similar without an account — but saving, rating, sections and recommendations need one. If you click Save to Library while signed out, the login box opens for you.
  • Once you're signed in, the app opens straight into My Library.
  • Forgot your username or password? Email us from the login box and we'll sort it out.
🔍 Searching for books
  • Pick a tab first — Title, Author or Genre — then type and press Search. Genre accepts loose terms like "cozy mystery" or "space opera".
  • Published between narrows results to a year range. Leave either box blank for an open-ended range, or both blank for all years.
  • Results are grouped by genre (each group is tinted by its genre) and numbered by rank. Use the page buttons at the bottom for more results.
  • The % badge on a card is how strongly that book matched — green is a strong match, amber medium, grey weak.
  • Click any card to expand it: cover art, the full description, action buttons, and links out to Google Books, Open Library, and a "Find Online" web search.
💾 Saving books to your library
  • Expand a book and click Save to Library. Saved books are what your recommendations are built from.
  • Open the My Library tab (far right) to see everything you saved. The switcher at the top flips between Saved, Liked and Disliked.
  • Remove on a saved book deletes it from your library and from any sections it was in. In the Liked/Disliked views, Remove just clears that rating.
📖 Reading status
  • Every saved book has a Status… dropdown: 📥 Want to Read, 📖 Reading, or ✅ Read. A book has one status at a time; pick No status to clear it.
  • The amber chips at the top of your library filter to each status, with a live count.
  • With a status chip open, the recommend button becomes Get Recommendations from "Reading" Books — a quick way to say "more like what I'm reading right now".
🗂️ Sections (your own shelves)
  • Click + New Section in your library and name it (e.g. "Sci-fi favorites"). A book can live in several sections at once.
  • In All Books, use the Add to section… dropdown on a book. Sections it's already in show a ✓.
  • With a section open, that dropdown becomes Move to section… (moves the book out of this one), and each card gains a Remove from "…" button.
  • Rename and Delete chips appear next to the section bar while a section is open. Deleting a section never deletes books — they stay in All Books.
✨ Getting recommendations
  • Whole library: in All Books, click Get Recommendations Based on My Library.
  • One section: open the section first — the button rescopes to just those books.
  • One reading status: open a status chip and use the same button.
  • A hand-picked set: tick the checkbox on any saved books; a Get Recommendations from N Selected Books bar appears. Best when your library spans moods and you want one of them.
  • Recommendations never include books already in your library or ones you've disliked — even when you scope to a single section.
  • ← Back to My Library returns you to where you were.
  • Few or no results usually means the source books have thin metadata, or your scope is very small. Save a few more books in that genre and retry.
🔗 Find Similar
  • Every book card — in search results, your library, or a recommendation list — has a Find Similar button that finds books like that one specific title. No account needed.
  • It's a one-book lookup, so unlike recommendations it ignores the rest of your library.
  • If a book has almost no description or genre data (common with webnovels and self-published serials), you'll get an honest empty result rather than unrelated books.
👍 👎 Teaching it your taste
  • 👍 tells it you want more books like this one; 👎 suppresses that kind of book and keeps it out of your results.
  • Click the same thumb again to undo the rating. Rated books show up under the Liked and Disliked views in your library.
  • You can rate a book without saving it — handy for pruning recommendations you don't want to see again.
🎨 Display & troubleshooting
  • The ☀️ / 🌙 button toggles light and dark mode; your choice is remembered on that device.
  • Recommendations are computed live from Google Books and Open Library, so the first run after changing your library takes a few seconds. Repeat runs are cached and near-instant.
  • Book data (covers, descriptions, genres) comes from those two providers — if a book looks sparse or mislabelled, that's usually the source data.
  • Something looks wrong? Email us from the link at the bottom of the page.
